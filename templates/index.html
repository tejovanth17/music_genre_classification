Pipeline

End-to-End Audio Intelligence

1. Acoustic Ingestion

Loads standard audio formats (.wav, .mp3, .flac) at 22,050 Hz, trims leading/trailing silent regions, and standardizes gain levels.

2. Feature Extraction

Computes 128-band Mel-Spectrograms, STFT matrices, 20 MFCCs, Spectral Centroids, Spectral Roll-off, and Harmonic/Percussive decompositions.

3. Prediction & Recommender

Evaluates deep convolutional tensor activations to output probability distributions and suggests sonically similar tracks via Cosine Similarity.
